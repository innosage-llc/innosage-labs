# Director gate

The selected reviewer is Astra HIGH acting as a cinematic adviser, not a claim of a real-world award. A score is editorial judgment, not a prediction of social performance.

For every exported candidate:

1. Record the export SHA256, video duration and codec details.
2. Extract and inspect the entire film at 2fps, plus a near-final frame. Inspect every sheet. Supplement uncertain transitions with more frames.
3. Analyze the complete audio separately. Do not claim synchronized playback. Do not send private video/audio to an external service without applicable authorization. A synthetic original score can be isolated from the video for audio review.
4. Score narrative/emotion /20, pacing/continuity /20, composition /15, sound-picture relationship /15, captions /10, distinctiveness /10, technical delivery /10. Give timestamped evidence for deductions.
5. Strictly greater than 90 and no hard failure is required. Missing evidence is N/A. Never infer a score from the target.
6. Revise failures, export a new file, and repeat the whole review. Keep earlier receipts. A changed hash invalidates the previous pass.
7. Ask the owner for review before issue closure or publication. Private review approval does not clear faces, plates, private information, music rights or third-party footage for public sharing.

Tool errors and sampled-frame reviews do not count as full accepted-method review. Technical checks do not replace editorial judgment. The example provides evidence-generation helpers, not an automated promise of 91-point films.
